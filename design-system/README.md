# Natally design system

Natally is a cute robot astrologer who reads a customer's natal chart aloud in a
candlelit atelier. The interface is a quiet night room: plum-black ground, warm candle
gold, cream text. It is dark only. There is no light theme.

This folder is the single source of colour, type, spacing, shape and motion.

| File | What it is |
|---|---|
| `tokens.json` | Colour, type, spacing and radius tokens, each with a usage note. |
| `tokens.extra.json` | Motion, layers, breakpoints and scene geometry. |
| `tokens.css` | Generated by `scripts/build-tokens.mjs` from the two files above. Never edited by hand. |
| `assets-manifest.json` | SHA-256 of every brand file under `public/brand/` and `public/atelier/`. |
| `components/` | One folder per component: guidelines and a live preview. |

The frozen screens that these tokens were taken from are in `LIBS/UI/STITCH/`.

## Colour

Three surface steps carry all depth: `canvas`, then `panel`, then `elevated`, each
outlined with the `border` hairline. There are no drop shadows, glows or gradient washes.

- Text is `cream` on every surface, `muted` for secondary lines.
- `gold` is scarce. An app screen has one solid gold button. Never make every label,
  border or link gold. The landing page is the exception: each selling-point section has
  its own gold purchase button.
- `coral` belongs to the customer: their name in the header and the edge of their chat
  bubble. `periwinkle` is for links inside readings. `violet` is for eyebrows only.
- Text on a gold fill is `on-gold`.

Contrast: `cream` on `canvas`, `panel` and `elevated` and `on-gold` on `gold` all exceed
4.5:1. `muted` is for text at 13px and above on `canvas` and `panel`.

## Type

| Family | Role |
|---|---|
| Fraunces 400 | Page headings, offer titles, prices, Natally's own words in the conversation. Never bold. |
| Cinzel 700, uppercase, wide tracking | The brand word NATALLY, READINGS BY NATALLY, section stamps. |
| Nunito Sans 400 and 700 | All body text, buttons, navigation. |
| JetBrains Mono 500 | Field labels, segmented tabs, degrees and coordinates, the version badge. |
| Natally Symbols | Astrological glyphs only. It sits in every font stack so a glyph never falls back to a colour emoji. |

All fonts are self-hosted from `public/fonts/` so the app renders the same offline.
Astrological glyphs are plain Unicode characters followed by the text-presentation
selector U+FE0E. No emoji anywhere.

## Shape and spacing

Spacing follows a 4px grid. Anything tappable is at least `touch-min` high and wide.
Inputs and secondary buttons are `control-height`; the gold primary button is
`primary-height`. Radii: `radius-control` for controls, `radius-card` for cards and
sheets, `radius-scene` for the atelier scene, `radius-pill` for chips and toggles.

## Motion

One easing, `ease-out`, for every transition. Views swap in `duration-swap`, the chart
inset moves in `duration-inset`, a highlighted placement pulses over `highlight-pulse`.
When the system asks for reduced motion, or the customer turns on Pause motion, nothing
loops and transitions are instant. Motion never blocks text or speech.

## The mascot and the room

Natally is one character everywhere: a silver chibi robot with amber eyes, lilac cheek
lights, a plum turban with a gold crescent-and-star brooch, and hands on a violet crystal
ball. Her artwork and the atelier room are brand files. They are used exactly as they
are: never redrawn, recoloured, cropped into a different character, or regenerated.

| File | Use |
|---|---|
| `public/atelier/room.webp` | The atelier scene, aspect 1586 by 992. |
| `public/atelier/poster.webp` | Static fallback for the scene. |
| `public/atelier/puppet-cleanplate.webp` | The room without head and arms, for animating the character over it. |
| `public/brand/natally-mascot.webp` | Animated mascot, 400 by 400. |

| `public/atelier/audio/*.mp3` | Room ambience and sound effects. |
| `public/brand/brand.json` | Her voice and character contract. |

## Voice and wording

Plain, warm and short. Natally's reading voice is intimate and alert and precise about
the actual chart. Product text never carries disclaimers, invented statistics, badges or
ratings. A price or a credit amount shown anywhere comes from configuration.
